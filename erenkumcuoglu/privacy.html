Privacy note.

This is a personal website. It collects as little as it can, and only to understand which pages people read.

What is measured. If you accept analytics, the site uses Google Analytics 4 to count page views, the pages you visit, roughly where you are (country and city), the device and browser you use, the site you came from, and a few clicks such as "Request a call". Google stores a cookie for this. IP addresses are not stored in full.

If you decline. No analytics cookie is set. Google may still receive an anonymous, cookieless signal that a page was viewed, with no identifier that could link visits to you.

What is not done. Nothing is used for advertising, nothing is sold, and no profile of you is built. Analytics data is kept for 14 months and then deleted by Google.

Booking a call. The "Request a call" button opens Cal.com in a new tab. What you enter there (name, email, company, your note) goes to Cal.com and to my calendar, and is used only to arrange and prepare the call.
